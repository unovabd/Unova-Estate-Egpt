# Manual Site Sheets vs Linked Milestone Billing

In many real estate companies, the construction and sales departments operate in silos. Site engineers log milestones (like roof castings or brickwork) on paper, but the billing desk only finds out weeks later, delaying collections.

### 1. The Billing Gap
Milestone-linked installments (e.g., "10% due on 5th floor casting") require prompt invoicing. A 10-day delay in billing slows down collections and affects construction progress.

### 2. Connected Milestones
Unova bridges this gap. When a site engineer marks a milestone as complete and uploads a photo validation via the mobile portal, the system automatically generates and sends the corresponding invoices to all linked buyers.

### 3. Live Project Visibility
This automation gives the accounts team instant updates, speeds up cash flow, and allows buyers to view progress photos on their self-service portal.
