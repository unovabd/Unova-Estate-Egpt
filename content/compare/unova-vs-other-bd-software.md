# Unova vs Other Regional Real Estate Software: What to Actually Check

Most real estate ERP and CRM options built for the regional market cover the same ground: a lead pipeline, unit inventory, installment schedules, and some form of construction/BOQ tracking. That's table stakes at this point — nearly every vendor in this space, Unova included, offers those.

The differences that actually matter show up once you're running the business, not during a feature-list comparison. Here's where to look closer with any vendor you're evaluating.

### 1. How Land Sold in Fractional Shares Actually Gets Handled
Egyptian land developers routinely sell land as fractional shares — Feddan divided ten ways among ten buyers. Ask any vendor: does the system auto-split cost and size across shares with exact reconciliation, and does a sold share's price permanently lock so re-pricing the rest never touches a past buyer's deal? Unova does both natively — it's not a workaround built on top of a generic "unit" object.

### 2. Whether Tax Deducted at Source / VAT Is a Real Workflow or a Checkbox
Under Egyptian tax rules, some transactions have the customer deposit VAT or withholding tax directly rather than through the seller. If a system only has one generic "VAT %" field, it can't distinguish these cases — which means the VAT liability and the books stop matching. Unova tracks tax withholding as its own path: certificate upload clears the liability, and a monthly tax return summary is computed straight from ledger transactions.

### 3. Whether a Landowner's Units Are *Actually* Excluded, or Just Hidden
In a JV project, a landowner's reserved units are compensation, not inventory. Ask whether that exclusion is enforced at the data layer — so a landowner unit can never accidentally enter the cost pool or the sale pipeline — or whether it's just a display filter that a sales team could still work around.

### 4. What's Actually Underneath the "Finance Module"
"Automated bookkeeping and invoicing" is not the same as a real general ledger. Unova runs on full double-entry accounting — every multi-line journal voucher is auto-decomposed into paired debit/credit rows, bank statements are imported and matched directly against the ledger, and AR/AP aging, trial balance, and balance sheet all come from that same ledger rather than a separate reporting layer bolted on top.

*Whichever vendor you're evaluating — Unova included — ask to see these four things live, not just described in a sales deck. That's where the real differences are.*
