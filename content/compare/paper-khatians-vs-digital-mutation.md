# Paper Khatians vs Digital Mutation Tracker

For land developers, tracking raw land purchases, mutations, and ownership chains (CS/RS/BRS Khatians) is critical. Relying on paper ledger books and physical document cabinets is a major operational risk.

### 1. Document Vulnerability
Paper deeds can be damaged, lost, or misplaced. Searching through land records for audits or disputes is a slow, manual process.

### 2. Cloud Archives
Unova provides a secure Cloud Document Locker. Developers can upload and organize CS/RS Khatians, deed records, and mutations, making them instantly searchable by Dag or Khatian number.

### 3. Integrated Plot Maps
Land mutations are linked directly to plot layout maps, providing an accurate, real-time overview of plot availability, booking statuses, and mutations.
