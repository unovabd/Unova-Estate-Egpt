# Unova vs Bitrix24: Dedicated Real Estate vs General Project Tools

Bitrix24 offers a wide array of tools, from chats to tasks. However, this broad focus results in a complex, cluttered interface that local sales executives and site engineers find difficult to adopt.

### 1. Simple, Role-Based Access
Bitrix24 can overwhelm staff with excessive menus. Unova provides simple, role-based portals: site engineers only see store inputs, sales reps only see lead tasks, and executives only see approvals.

### 2. Real Estate Workflows
Bitrix24 has no native understanding of property developer needs. Unova manages post-dated check vaults, automated collection schedules, and joint-venture splits out-of-the-box.

### 3. Local Customer Support
Instead of dealing with international support tickets, Unova provides hands-on, local training and support based in Dhaka to ensure successful adoption.
