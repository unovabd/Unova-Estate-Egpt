# Unova vs Zoho CRM: Why Generic CRM Fails Real Estate Developers

Implementing an enterprise software system for real estate operations requires a deep understanding of industry-specific workflows. While Zoho CRM is an excellent, flexible CRM for generic sales pipelines, it falls short when applied to property developer operations.

Real estate sales aren't transactional; they are multi-year partnerships involving bookings, down payments, post-dated check clearance, landowner allocations, and registration milestones.

### 1. Installment & Collection Gaps
In Zoho CRM, generating a 48-month payment schedule requires custom Deluge scripting or third-party billing extensions. Unova, however, features a dedicated installment manager that automatically generates schedules based on flat cost, down-payment, and custom milestones.

### 2. Landowner Share Split
Joint-venture land development is a staple of the Bangladeshi real estate market. Dividing apartment shares between landowners and the builder is completely automated in Unova, whereas Zoho has no concept of structural property split allocations.

### 3. Localized Integrations
Unova comes pre-integrated with Bangladeshi SMS gateways (e.g., Greenweb, Teletalk) and mobile payment channels, making it a ready-to-run solution for local real estate operators.
