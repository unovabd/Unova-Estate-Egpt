# Physical Cheque Registers vs Automated PDC Clearing Alerts

Managing post-dated cheques (PDCs) from dozens of buyers is a major operational challenge. If a cheque is deposited late, it delays construction cash flow; if deposited early without buyer confirmation, it bounces, straining client relations.

### 1. Manual PDC Risks
Relying on staff to check paper folders and calendar dates daily leaves room for error. A single missed deposit can delay subcontractor payments.

### 2. Automated Clearing Dashboards
Unova features a dedicated PDC Vault. The dashboard lists upcoming clearances 7 to 10 days in advance and sends automated WhatsApp reminders to buyers to ensure their accounts are funded.

### 3. Accounting Integration
Upon clearance, the accountant clicks 'Approve', and Unova automatically creates the bank credit vouchers and updates the customer ledger.
