# Unova vs HubSpot: Visual Inventory vs Inbound Marketing

HubSpot is a world-class inbound marketing tool, ideal for tracking customer journeys. However, a real estate company needs more than marketing: it needs accounting, store inventories, and client portals.

### 1. Inbound Marketing vs Core Operations
HubSpot does a great job of capturing leads, but once a lead books an apartment, HubSpot's capabilities end. It cannot track physical store consumption, process running bills for sub-contractors, or manage balance sheets.

### 2. Integrated Cost Centers
Unova integrates your marketing CRM directly with double-entry accounting ledgers. When material is released from the site store, the project's cost center is updated immediately.

### 3. Local Gateway Support
HubSpot supports international payment gateways. Unova is tailored for Egypt & MENA, supporting local channels like Fawry, InstaPay, and Paymob for installment collections.
