# How Sales Automation Boosts Housing Developer Revenue by 20%

In the competitive property development market, speed is everything. When a lead submits an enquiry about a 3-bedroom apartment, the developer who contacts them first closes the deal. Yet, many housing companies take days to assign and contact their prospects.

Implementing **Sales Automation** is the fastest way to increase conversion rates and boost sales revenue.

---

## 1. Eliminate Lead Leakage
Lead leakage occurs when an executive forgets to follow up, or a contact sheet gets lost. An automated CRM syncs directly with:
* **Facebook Ads & Forms**
* **WhatsApp API enquiries**
* **Website landing pages**

Once captured, the lead is automatically assigned based on a round-robin routing algorithm. The agent receives an instant notification, keeping response times under 5 minutes.

---

## 2. Dynamic Payment Installment Schedules
Real estate sales are not one-time transactions. Booking a property involves booking money, signing agreements, and installments.
Automation helps you:
* Create a payment plan in 2 clicks.
* Set up automated SMS alerts via local mobile gateways (like Teletalk or GP) 3 days before an installment is due.
* Track payment statuses in real-time.

---

## 3. Real-Time Inventory Control
One of the biggest issues in real estate sales is double-selling. If two agents sell the same commercial floor to different clients, it creates a massive legal and PR headache.

Sales automation features a **live property grid**. When an agent locks a unit, it immediately shows as "Reserved" for all other team members, ensuring zero conflicts.
