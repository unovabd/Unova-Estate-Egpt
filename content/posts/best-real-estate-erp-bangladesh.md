# Best Real Estate ERP Software in Bangladesh

Managing a real estate development company in Bangladesh is a complex operation. Between land procurement pipelines, structural engineering bills of quantities (BOQ), sales booking installments, RA contractor billing, and customer relations, developers often struggle to maintain a unified database.

That is why choosing the right **Real Estate ERP Software** is critical to your company's scaling strategy.

---

## The Challenges of Traditional Management in Dhaka

Most property developers in Dhaka and Chittagong rely on generic accounting software combined with a massive web of Microsoft Excel sheets. This approach causes several problems:
1. **Lead Leakage:** Sales agents forget to follow up with potential buyers.
2. **Double Selling:** Units are accidentally booked by two different agents.
3. **Budget Overruns:** Procurement managers buy cement or rod without proper budget approvals, causing high material variance.
4. **Instalment Delays:** Customers miss installments due to lack of automated SMS or payment alerts.

---

## Key Features to Look For in a Real Estate ERP

If you are looking for an ERP to run your housing or land business in Bangladesh, it must include these core features:

### 1. Unified Lead & CRM Pipeline
A robust real estate CRM must track all enquiries from walkthroughs, website forms, and Facebook Lead forms. It must assign leads to agents dynamically and log every phone call or meeting.

### 2. Installment Management with Local Gateway Integration
Since most buyers in Bangladesh purchase flats or plots through multi-year installment schedules, the ERP must automate installment generation, alert users when payments are due, and generate printed money receipts.

### 3. Construction Cost Cost Control (BOQ & Store Inventory)
Your site engineers must be able to log daily progress reports, material consumption, and request store issues. The ERP must compare actual material use against the initial Bill of Quantities (BOQ) budget.
