# What is an ERP System and When Does a Real Estate Company Need One?

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) is a centralized database software system that integrates all core departments of a business—Sales CRM, Land Feasibility, Procurement, Contractor Billing, HR/Payroll, and Finance—into one unified platform.

---

## Key Signs Your Real Estate Company Needs an ERP

* **Departmental Silos:** Sales, engineering, and accounts operate on separate spreadsheets, causing data mismatches.
* **Delayed Financial Closing:** Taking days or weeks to prepare monthly P&L and project cash flow statements.
* **Uncontrolled Site Costs:** Material consumption exceeding BOQ estimates without prior warning.
* **Installment Collection Delays:** Missing collection dates due to unorganized manual tracking.
