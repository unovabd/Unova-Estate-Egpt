# Custom vs. Ready-Made Software: Which is Best for Your Business?

When adopting enterprise software, real estate leaders face a critical decision: should you commission **Custom Software** built from scratch, or license **Ready-Made Software** (Off-the-shelf Software)?

Both options carry distinct advantages and limitations. Your decision depends on company scale, budget, and long-term expansion plans. Here is a comprehensive comparison.

---

## What is Custom Software?
Custom software is engineered from scratch to match a company's specific operational rules and workflows.

### Advantages of Custom Software:
* **100% Tailored Fit:** Every module aligns with your company processes without bloat.
* **Full Ownership:** Source code and intellectual property remain with your business without ongoing license dependencies.
* **Seamless Internal Integration:** Easily connects to existing legacy infrastructure.
* **Long-Term Flexibility:** Modify or expand features as your business scales.

### Disadvantages of Custom Software:
* **High Upfront Capital:** Significant initial development cost.
* **Time to Deploy:** Development cycles take 6 to 12 months.
* **Maintenance Overhead:** Requires dedicated in-house IT engineers or external development retainers.

---

## What is Ready-Made Software?
Ready-made software refers to pre-built platforms accessible instantly via subscription or license models (e.g., standard CRMs or accounting tools).

### Advantages of Ready-Made Software:
* **Low Initial Capital:** Low upfront entry cost on a monthly/annual subscription.
* **Instant Deployment:** Ready to use immediately without waiting for development.
* **Vendor Maintained:** Software updates, security patches, and cloud maintenance are managed by the vendor.

### Disadvantages of Ready-Made Software:
* **Rigid Customization:** Operational processes must adapt to the software's hardcoded logic.
* **Unneeded Bloat:** Paying for features your team never uses.

---

## Quick Comparison Overview

| Parameter | Custom Software | Ready-Made Software |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Upfront Investment** | High | Low |
| **Time to Live** | 6 - 12 Months | Instant |
| **Customization** | 100% Flexible | Limited |
| **Code Ownership** | Proprietary | Vendor Licensed |
| **Maintenance** | In-House / Retainer | Vendor SLA |
