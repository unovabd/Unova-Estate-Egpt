# What is a Real Estate CRM and Why is it Vital for Growing Property Developers?

Customer Relationship Management (CRM) software is the engine of modern property sales. Unlike generic CRMs, a specialized **Real Estate CRM** manages the unique buyer journey of property acquisition.

---

## Core Capabilities of a Real Estate CRM

1. **Centralized Lead Repository:** Automatically gathers leads from Meta ads, property portals, website forms, and walk-ins.
2. **Instant Lead Assignment:** Uses round-robin logic to assign new inquiries to active sales agents within seconds.
3. **Omnichannel Communication Logs:** Unifies WhatsApp chats, SMS follow-ups, email threads, and call notes into a single buyer timeline.
4. **Visual Sales Pipeline:** Drag-and-drop Kanban tracking from initial inquiry down to booking fee payment.
5. **Real-Time Analytics:** Tracks agent response speeds, site visit ratios, and marketing campaign ROI.
