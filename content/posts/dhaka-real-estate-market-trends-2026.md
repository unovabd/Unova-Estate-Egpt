# Dhaka Real Estate Market Trends & Tech Adoption

The Dhaka property market is undergoing a major shift. Rapid urbanization in areas like Uttara Third Phase, Purbachal, and Bashundhara has increased the demand for both mid-range and luxury residential projects.

However, developers are facing rising land costs and interest rates, which squeeze profit margins. To adapt, leading builders are replacing slow manual operations with automation.
