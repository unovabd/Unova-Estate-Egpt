## 1. Overview

Real estate finance departments face distinct operational challenges:
1. **Multi-Year Instalment Tracking:** Real estate sales generate long-term customer receivables. Manually matching bank deposits with PDC cheque registers causes financial reporting delays.
2. **NBR Source Tax Complexity:** Computing Tax Deducted at Source (AIT) and VAT on contractor bills and land purchases without automated rules increases audit risk.
3. **Project Cost Overruns:** Without real-time integration between site material purchases and accounts ledgers, project budget overruns are discovered months too late.

Unova Finance ERP resolves these vulnerabilities with automated tax rules, bank reconciliation engines, and real-time project profitability dashboards.

## 2. Key Features & Modules

### A. Real Estate Chart of Accounts & Project P&L
Organize financial ledgers by project and building phase:

* **Project-Wise Cost Centers:** Track land acquisition costs, architectural design fees, site material purchases, and sales commissions separately for each project.
* **Real-Time P&L Statements:** Generate instant income and expenditure reports for individual building projects or consolidated enterprise views.

### B. NBR AIT & VAT Source Tax Processor
Automate tax compliance according to Bangladesh National Board of Revenue rules:

* **Contractor Bill Deductions:** Auto-calculate AIT and VAT percentages on contractor RA bills before routing net payments.
* **Challan & Tax Audit Vault:** Store electronic tax deduction certificates and challan receipt logs for fast audit verification.

### C. PDC Cheque Vault & Bank Reconciliation
Streamline cash and bank operations:

* **PDC Clearing Dashboard:** View daily post-dated cheques due for bank deposit with automated reminder alerts.
* **Automated Bank Reconciliation:** Import bank statements (CSV/Excel) to auto-match customer deposits against active payment ledgers.
