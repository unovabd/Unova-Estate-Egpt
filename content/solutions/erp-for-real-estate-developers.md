## 1. Overview

The Egyptian housing and property development sector operates under distinct structural challenges:
1. **Multi-Year Financial Lifecycles:** Real estate transactions span 3 to 5 years of regular monthly or quarterly instalments. Manually tracking post-dated cheques (PDC) and interest penalties without automated software leads to revenue leakage.
2. **Joint-Venture Allocation Conflicts:** Landowner vs. developer unit sharing ratios must be tracked with strict legal precision to avoid disputes during booking and handover.
3. **Site Expenditure Waste:** Construction material wastage on building sites can erode up to 12% of total project profit margins if store requisitions are not reconciled against baseline Bill of Quantities (BOQ) estimates.

Unova Estate addresses these core vulnerabilities through automated data pipelines, role-based security, and real-time executive dashboards.

## 2. Key Features & Modules

### A. Land Acquisition & Joint-Venture Management
The land module allows developers to record raw land purchases, joint-venture deed terms, mutation status, and Khatian records. 

* **Landowner Allotment Matrix:** Auto-assign specific apartment units or land plots to the landowner according to the agreed ratio (e.g., 50:50 or 40:60).
* **Automated Deed Locks:** Allotted landowner units are instantly locked from the public sales inventory, eliminating accidental bookings by sales representatives.

### B. Dynamic Inventory Locker & Pricing Engine
Managing apartment inventory across multiple sales offices, property agents, and digital campaigns demands instant synchronization.

* **Live Inventory Map:** Sales executives view color-coded status maps (Available, On-Hold, Booked, Registered, Handed Over) on their mobile devices.
* **Instant Unit Locking:** When an executive receives a booking token, they can apply a temporary 48-hour lock on the unit directly from the app.
* **Dynamic Price & Payment Plan Generator:** Automatically compute down payments, monthly instalment amounts, utility connection fees, and car parking charges in seconds.

### C. Instalment Collection & PDC Cheque Tracking
Collection efficiency directly determines project cashflow health. Unova ERP automates customer debt ledgers and payment alerts:

* **Official WhatsApp & SMS Automation:** Automated payment alerts are sent 5 days prior to instalment due dates, reducing default rates by up to 35%.
* **PDC Ledger Management:** Record post-dated cheque numbers, bank names, and maturity dates. Receive daily automated reminders for cheque deposits.
* **Bounced Cheque Penalty Processor:** If a cheque bounces, the system auto-posts a bounced status to the customer's ledger, applies configurable penalty fees, and notifies the accounts department.

## 3. How It Works

Unova ERP bridges the gap between the corporate head office, field site engineers, and sales teams.

### Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Budget Enforcement
Upload approved architectural and structural BOQ estimates (cement bags, steel rebar tonnage, sand CFT, tiles, electrical fittings).

* **Requisition Vs Budget Locks:** When a site engineer submits a Material Requisition (MR), the system compares requested quantities against the remaining project BOQ. Requests exceeding baseline estimates are automatically flagged for Manager approval.
* **Store Issue Ledger:** Real-time tracking of site inventory balances ensures zero material theft or unaccounted stock usage.
* **Contractor RA Bill Verification:** Contractors submit Running Account bills based on completed site measurements. Unova ERP automatically deducts security deposits, advance recoveries, and source tax (AIT/VAT) before routing net payments to accounting.
