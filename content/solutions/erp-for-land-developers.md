## 1. Overview

The land development sector in Bangladesh operates under specific operational rules:
1. **Complex Deed Lineage:** Raw land purchases require tracking CS, SA, RS, and BRS Khatian records, Porcha documents, and mutation status to prevent legal ownership disputes.
2. **Long-Term Instalment Plans:** Land buyers frequently purchase plots on 3 to 10-year monthly or quarterly instalment plans. Managing Post-Dated Cheques (PDC) and tracking arrears requires automated ledger alerts.
3. **Landowner Ratio Allocation:** Joint-venture land development deeds specify exact plot allotment ratios between landowners and the developer.

Unova Land ERP addresses these challenges with automated land deed registries, visual plot lockers, and automated instalment collection pipelines.

## 2. Key Features & Modules

### A. Khatian & Land Deed Vault
Digitize every acre of land acquired for project development:

* **Deed Lineage Mapping:** Record seller names, deed numbers, sub-registry office details, CS/SA/RS/BRS Khatian numbers, and Dag numbers.
* **Mutation Status Alerts:** Track land mutation applications at Namjari offices with real-time status progression logs.

### B. Visual Plot Layout Mapper & Inventory Locker
Transform physical layout plans into interactive digital plot maps:

* **Block & Plot Grid:** Categorize plots by Block (Block A, B, C), Road Width (25 ft, 30 ft, 40 ft), Facing (South, North-East), and Size (3 Katha, 5 Katha, 10 Katha).
* **Real-time Inventory Locker:** Sales executives view live plot status (Available, On-Hold, Booked, Registered, Allotted to Landowner). Applying a booking lock immediately updates all sales channels.

### C. Instalment Collection & PDC Cheque Ledger
Automate long-term land buyer collections:

* **WhatsApp & SMS Notices:** Send automated payment reminders 5 days prior to instalment due dates.
* **PDC Deposit Ledger:** Track post-dated cheques with maturity alerts and bounced cheque penalty interest processing.
