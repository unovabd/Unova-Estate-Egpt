## 1. Overview

Construction site management in Egypt faces distinct operational challenges:
1. **Material Wastage & Leakage:** Excess cement, rebar tonnage, or tiles ordered beyond BOQ baseline budgets directly shrink developer profit margins.
2. **Contractor Billing Disputes:** Manual measurement sheets lead to disputes with civil, electrical, and plumbing contractors regarding work completion percentages.
3. **Site-to-Head-Office Silos:** Site engineers working offline create delays in material procurement and vendor invoice processing.

Unova Construction ERP resolves these vulnerabilities with automated BOQ locks, mobile store requisitions, and automated contractor billing engines.

---

## 2. Key Features & Modules

### A. Bill of Quantities (BOQ) Budget Engine
Enforce strict budget limits from day one:

* **BOQ Upload:** Import structural and architectural estimates (cement bags, steel rebar tons, sand CFT, tiles sq.ft).
* **Automated Requisition Locks:** Material requisitions exceeding remaining BOQ budgets are flagged for Director approval.

### B. Site Store & Goods Received Ledger
Control raw material inventory across building sites:

* **Store Receipts (GRN):** Storekeepers log incoming material deliveries, verifying supplier delivery chalans against purchase orders.
* **Store Issue Tracking:** Track raw materials issued to sub-contractors with automated gate passes to prevent theft.

### C. Contractor Running Account (RA) Billing
Automate contractor measurement verifications:

* **Milestone Billing Verification:** Sub-contractors submit RA bills based on completed site measurements.
* **Automated Deductions:** System auto-deducts advance payments, security deposit retentions (e.g. 10%), and NBR source tax (AIT/VAT).
